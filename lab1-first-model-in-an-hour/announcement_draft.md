## LinkedIn post
A while back I promised teachers a free lab for Hour of AI: students build their first machine learning model in about an hour. It's ready, just in time for CS Education Week (Dec 8-14).

"Your First AI Model in an Hour" is a Google Colab notebook for students aged roughly 13-18. No ML background and very little Python needed. In about 45-60 minutes, they:

• learn what a model actually is (no magic, just lots of small corrections)
• explore a real dataset of 70,000 clothing images
• build and train a small neural network in PyTorch and watch the loss drop
• test it on images it's never seen, then run their own experiments
• and, most importantly, find where it fails: shirts it can't tell apart, and a boot it confidently calls a bag once you change the background

That last part is the point. I want students to leave knowing how to build AI and how to question it: what data it learned from, who's missing, and when it shouldn't be trusted.

It runs on free Colab CPU with no installs, and comes with a short teacher guide: timing plan, discussion questions, common hiccups, and extensions for fast finishers.

It's free, and always will be. If you teach, please try it with your class and tell me how it goes.
