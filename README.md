# Hour of AI

Hour of AI is a free hour for students to learn how artificial intelligence actually works. These two labs are classroom materials for Computer Science Education Week, December 8–14, 2026.

They are written for students roughly ages 13–18. Each one takes about 45–60 minutes. Everything runs in the browser on free [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/). There is nothing to install, and there are no API keys. A Google account is enough. A school Workspace account works too, if your admin has turned Colab on.

If you teach, start with the teacher guide. It has a timing plan, discussion questions, and a short list of things that sometimes go wrong. Then send students the notebook.

## Lab 1: Your First AI Model in an Hour

Students train a real image classifier on photos of clothing. They watch it learn, test it on pictures it has never seen, change one setting, and then find where it fails and why. No machine learning background is needed, and little or no Python.

## Lab 2: How Chatbots Really Work

Students open a small, real language model. They watch it predict the next word, turn its creativity up and down, see how a word predictor becomes a chatbot, and catch it inventing facts. Lab 1 is a nice companion, and this lab also stands on its own. Still no API keys.
